# Recipes

**This folder is empty on purpose.** A recipe here is shared content, so it needs a qualified reviewer, a nutrition source with its version, access date and written licence evidence, and names and steps in English, Malay and Tamil. None of that exists yet, and nothing may be invented or copied. See `docs/food/recipes.md`.

- `<id>.json` is one recipe (type `Recipe` in `src/food/recipes.ts`), shared only (no `userId`).
- Check with `npm run recipes:check`. Nothing published is reported as a launch gate, not a failure.
